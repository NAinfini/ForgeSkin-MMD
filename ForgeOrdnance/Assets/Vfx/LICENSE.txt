Forge effect library effects
=================================================

forge-ordnance-fx.bundle (when installed) holds the effects of the carrier, Arsenal and the exosuits, rebuilt from Unity Asset Store packs under
the Standard Unity Asset Store EULA, which allows shipping them inside a built product. They are shipped only inside that built bundle; the packs'
source files are not redistributed and are not in this repository. fx-licences.forge next to the bundle lists every pack used, its recorded
licence and the effects taken from it; forge-ordnance-fx.manifest.json is its build receipt.
The shaders that draw them (Tools/Vfx/ForgeVfx/Assets/ForgeVfx/Shaders) are the project's own, MIT.

HeavyBlast/forge-visual-fx.bundle
---------------------------------
The heavy grenade launcher's explosion. Built with Unity 2019.4.21f1 by the effect library (Tools/Vfx/FxLibrary/fx_slots.py); it holds
effects rebuilt from Unity Asset Store packs under the Standard Unity Asset Store EULA, which allows shipping them inside a built product.
They are shipped only inside that built bundle; the packs' source files are not redistributed and are not in this repository.
HeavyBlast/fx-licences.forge next to the bundle lists every pack used, its recorded licence and the effects taken from it;
HeavyBlast/forge-visual-fx.manifest.json is its build receipt. ForgeWeapon_EnergyLab ships its own copy of the same bundle.
